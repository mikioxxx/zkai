Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single sprite atlas for hidden Easter eggs in a green cyberpunk website. Portrait 1024x1536 canvas, pure black background. Three equally tall horizontal cells, no separators. Each cell has one centered minimal neon-green etched emblem with wide black margins, each emblem about 280px wide and 280px tall: TOP a hippopotamus head with round ears and broad snout; MIDDLE Thomas the Tank Engine, front-facing small steam engine with his recognizable round friendly face, funnel and subtle number 1; BOTTOM Kagawa's Udon Nou mascot, white rounded head with open top showing coiled udon noodle brain, tiny dot eyes and small mouth, miniature stubby body. All three rendered exclusively muted green outline engravings on black, consistent sparse strong linework readable when shrunk to 30 pixels. No text, labels, frames, scenery, watermark, or extra characters. These are tiny secret industrial markings, not prominent cartoon portraits.
